About

Born in Romania.
Rebuilt in Miami.

“With what we have.”

I cook the way my family taught me: nothing wasted, everything loved. Half an onion and yesterday's bread were never leftovers. They were tomorrow's dinner.

Fine-dining kitchens taught me precision. Food waste taught me purpose. Now I build technology with the same respect for every ingredient, so anyone can open their fridge and cook something beautiful.
